NOTIFICATION INTEGRATION
================================================================================

Settings now control notification creation:

1. "City Searched" notification
   - Checks: settings.notificationsEnabled

2. "Rain Expected Tomorrow" notification
   - Checks: settings.notificationsEnabled AND settings.rainAlertsEnabled

3. "High Temperature Alert" notification
   - Checks: settings.notificationsEnabled AND settings.weatherAlertsEnabled

4. "Weather API Error" notification
   - Checks: settings.notificationsEnabled AND settings.weatherAlertsEnabled

If alerts are disabled, notifications won't be created (not just hidden).
